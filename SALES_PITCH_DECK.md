## Part 3: Handling Tough Judge Questions (Q&A Defense Guide)

### Question 1: "Why would an enterprise trust an AI bot to talk to their clients without hallucinating wrong prices?"
- **Answer**:
  > *"That is precisely why we engineered our dual-layer governance: First, system prompts enforce strict deterministic bounding based on the client's uploaded PDF catalog—if an item isn't in the catalog, the bot escalates. Second, our Human-in-the-Loop threshold automatically locks any quotation over ₹5 Lakhs for human one-click approval before sending. The AI drafts; the human verifies when stakes are high."*

### Question 2: "What is your defensibility against OpenAI or WhatsApp releasing their own agent tools?"
- **Answer**:
  > *"OpenAI provides generic models; WhatsApp provides raw APIs. Neither provides the verticalized integration, local ERP sync (Tally, Zoho, SAP), pre-built proforma quoting logic, and omnichannel triage that Indian SMBs require. Our moat is domain-specific operational workflows and local industrial presence."*

### Question 3: "How do you guarantee sub-200ms latency on Indian mobile networks?"
- **Answer**:
  > *"We utilize Groq's custom LPU (Language Processing Unit) architecture, which achieves 500+ tokens per second, eliminating the 2-second cold-start delay of traditional GPU clusters. Additionally, our frontend utilizes Next.js streaming Server-Sent Events, meaning the user sees the first token in under 190ms, before the sentence even finishes generating."*
