## 2. Multi-Channel Go-To-Market (GTM) Playbook

### Channel 1: LinkedIn B2B Founder-Led Authority Campaign
- **Target Audience**: Managing Directors, CEOs, VP of Sales in Indian MSMEs (Manufacturing, Logistics, Real Estate).
- **Post Structure 1 (The Polarizing Problem)**:
  > *"Most Indian MSME owners think they have a lead generation problem. You don't. You have a **Lead Suffocation Problem**.*
  > 
  > *Last month, we audited an Ahmedabad manufacturing client spending ₹2.5 Lakhs/month on Google & Meta Ads.*
  > *Their leads were waiting an average of 4.2 HOURS for a quote on WhatsApp.*
  > *By the time their executive called back, 68% of callers said: 'Sorry, we already placed the order with another vendor.'*
  > 
  > *We plugged in Upteky NexusAI.*
  > *Response time went from 4.2 hours $\rightarrow$ 8 seconds.*
  > *Recovered ₹32 Lakhs in lost orders in 60 days.*
  > 
  > *Speed is the ultimate sales pitch. Comment 'AUDIT' and we'll analyze your response time for free."*

### Channel 2: High-Converting WhatsApp B2B Conversational Funnel
- **Trigger**: Inbound click from Meta ads or website QR code: *"Test the Upteky NexusAI Live Bot"*.
- **Autonomous Flow**:
  1. *NexusAI*: *"Namaste! 👋 Welcome to Upteky NexusAI. I am an autonomous agent running on Groq LPU hardware. Tell me: Which industry is your business in? (1) Manufacturing (2) Logistics (3) Real Estate (4) Other"*
  2. *User*: *"1"*
  3. *NexusAI*: *"Excellent. What is your biggest operational headache today? A) Slow quotation turnaround B) Missed weekend leads C) High support staffing costs"*
  4. *User*: *"A"*
  5. *NexusAI*: *"Understood. For manufacturing clients like JM PlastoPack, NexusAI drafts instant HSN/GST-compliant proforma quotations directly in chat. Would you like me to generate a sample 1-page ROI projection for your company? Type your monthly lead volume."*

### Channel 3: Industrial Estate & Chamber of Commerce Expo Blitz (Gujarat Focus)
- **Target Zones**: Naroda GIDC, Vatva GIDC, Changodar, Sanand, Hazira.
- **Tactic**: Set up an interactive "Speed-to-Lead Challenge" booth at industrial expos.
  - Visitors scan a QR code with their phone and send an inquiry to a live demo bot.
  - An overhead display timer shows the response arriving in under 2 seconds with accurate technical specs.
  - **Hook**: *"If your sales team can't beat our bot, you're losing deals every single day."*
