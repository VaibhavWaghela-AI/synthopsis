## Long (Show HN / Devpost / Reddit r/SideProject)
Title: Show HN: Synthopsis – Autonomous B2B Multi-Agent Orchestration with Visual DAGs & Tally Prime ERP Integration

Traditional enterprise B2B sales inquiries take an average of 4 hours per inquiry: sales reps manually decipher informal WhatsApp messages or email PDFs, calculate pricing matrices, check credit terms, and re-type the voucher into Tally or SAP.

We built Synthopsis to eliminate this bottleneck completely:

1. Unified Omnichannel Ingestion: Captures RFQs across WhatsApp Cloud Webhooks, IMAP email streams, and web chats.
2. Dual-Engine Intelligence: Combines ultra-low latency Groq LPU (Llama 3.3 70B @ 500 tok/s) for sub-second quote extraction with DeepSeek-R1 / Gemini for complex multi-tier margin validation.
3. Visual Blueprint DAG: Interactive node-graph builder with real-time execution tracing, conditional deal-size routing, and human-in-the-loop escalation.
4. Native Indian Enterprise ERP Integration: Directly generates verified XML vouchers ready for Tally Prime and SAP S/4HANA with full DPDP Act 2023 compliance.

Stack: Next.js 16 (App Router, Tailwind, Lucide), FastAPI (Python 3.12, Uvicorn), Groq LPU SDK, ReactFlow DAG engine.
